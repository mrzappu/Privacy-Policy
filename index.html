Introduction

Infinity Music is a bot that operates on Discord to provide music playback and related features. Protecting your privacy is important to us. This policy describes what information we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

The types of data we may collect include:

  • Discord profile information: username, unique Discord ID, and avatar (if available).
  • Command and chat logs: text commands and optional messages sent to the bot for operation and debugging.
  • Server data: the IDs of servers (guilds) where the bot is installed and any server-specific settings.
  • Technical data: usage metrics, error logs, and performance data collected to keep the bot running smoothly.
  • Optional data: any additional information you provide (for example, playlists or metadata) will only be stored if you submit it.

How We Use Data

We use collected data for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the bot (e.g., execute commands and play music).
  • To improve the bot’s performance, troubleshoot issues, and analyze usage.
  • To store server-specific settings and preferences.
  • We will not use your personal data for marketing without your explicit consent.

Third-Party Services

Infinity Music may rely on third-party services for features such as audio hosting, databases, or logging. These services may process data under their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party services we use.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, such as access controls and monitoring. However, no internet-connected system can be 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Request deletion of personal data (subject to legal obligations to retain certain records).

To make a request, please use the contact information below and include your Discord ID (for example: 123456789012345678) so we can locate your records.

Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations. Some logs may be kept for a limited period (for example, 90 days) unless a longer retention is required for security or legal reasons.

Children

This bot is not intended for use by children under the applicable age threshold without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date below.
